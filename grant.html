What we are building

Reusable Solana infrastructure for transparent voluntary community contributions and campaign pages.

Current stage

Working prototype with SOL QR payments, wallet flow, public read-only transaction tracking and social promotion.

The problem

Small community projects and creators often lack simple crypto contribution infrastructure. Traditional fundraising tools can add payment friction, geographic restrictions and centralised dependencies. DealDost explores a Solana-native approach where contributions settle directly on-chain and activity can be independently verified.

Why Solana?

Solana is the core settlement and verification layer. DealDost is designed specifically around SOL payments, Solana wallets, QR payments and Solana mainnet transaction data. The goal is to demonstrate a practical community use case built around the network rather than treating crypto as an optional payment method.

Public-good commitment

Reusable technical components and documentation developed through the grant will be open-sourced where practical. The aim is to let other Solana builders learn from and deploy similar transparent contribution infrastructure.

DealDost will not custody supporter funds and will never request seed phrases, private keys or wallet passwords. Contributions are voluntary and are not investments, securities or guaranteed-return arrangements.
